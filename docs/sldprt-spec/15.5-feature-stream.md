# 15.5 `Contents/Config-0-ResolvedFeatures`: the feature tree

The feature stream is the second half of the `CArchive` that `Contents/Config-0`
starts (15.0.4, 15.4): it is assembled after `config-0.srl`, with that listing's
archive, and its class records and references count on from where `Config-0` ended.
It holds the template's folders, planes and origin, then the design's sketches and
extrusions, in tree order: corner rectangles each extruded blind; or one such box, a fillet on
its four edges along its depth, a circle cut through all in both directions and a fillet on the
hole's edge, each but the box optional. Uncompressed, not wrapped in ZLB.

| Listing | What it gives |
|---|---|
| `config-0-resolvedfeatures.srl` | the member: header, the template's records, the loop over `features`, the end |
| `config-0-resolvedfeatures-fixed.srl` | the template's records, Comments to Origin, and the history entries (15.5.3) |
| `config-0-resolvedfeatures-sketch.srl` | one corner rectangle sketch (15.5.7) |
| `config-0-resolvedfeatures-extrude.srl` | one blind boss extrusion (15.5.8) |
| `config-0-resolvedfeatures-circle.srl` | one circle sketch (15.5.12) |
| `config-0-resolvedfeatures-cut.srl` | one cut through all in both directions (15.5.13) |
| `config-0-resolvedfeatures-fillet.srl` | one fillet of constant radius (15.5.14) |
| `config-0-macros.srl` | the feature record, the sketch point records, the face lists and their entries, a dimension's display and an annotation view (15.4.2, 15.4.9, 15.5.7, 15.5.8, 15.5.13, 15.5.14) |

| Part | Bytes | Typed | Hex | Slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| o01 | 5,555 | 5,041 (90.7%) | 0 | 514 (9.3%) |
| o02 | 11,850 | 10,673 (90.1%) | 39 (0.3%) | 1,138 (9.6%) |
| o12 | 17,154 | 15,373 (89.6%) | 75 (0.4%) | 1,706 (9.9%) |

Slot bytes include every field an expression gives (ids, flags that follow the
design) and every `objref`. Typed bytes outside the named fields are a typed reading
of constants, as in 15.4.

## 15.5.1 Framing

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| u32 | the archive's next index after `Config-0`: `@archive set header` (README 15.0.3) takes it, `u32 $_archive.header` writes it. 101 in o01, 109 in o02, 110 in o12 |
| u16 | the number of top-level features, `=17+len($features)` |
| | the template's records (15.5.3), then for each item of `features` a corner rectangle (15.5.7), a circle (15.5.12), a boss extrusion (15.5.8), a cut (15.5.13) or a fillet (15.5.14) |
| u32 | 0, the end of the list |

VERIFIED (5 parts; the header u32 in 13 streams of 12 parts, 15.0.4). The header's
17 counts the template's records; the listing names 16 top-level records (15.5.3)
and which one counts twice, or what else is counted, is UNKNOWN. The count is
`17 + len(features)` in all five parts, and in o11 (23: six features, its fillets
counted; VERIFIED). A circle and its cut count as two items like a rectangle and its
extrusion, and a fillet as one.

## 15.5.2 The `features` slot

`features` lists the design's features after the template's, in tree order: every
sketch is followed at once by the extrusion that uses it, and a fillet comes after the
extrusion or cut whose body it changes (15.6.2). Its schema is the one of section 15.6.2,
shared by every section. This section reads these fields:

| Item | Fields read here |
|---|---|
| sketch | `name`, `sketch`, `circle`, `id`, `time`, `created`, `regen`, `cache_memo`; a rectangle also `corners`, `at_origin`; a circle `centre`, `start` |
| extrusion | `name`, `sketch`, `fillet`, `cut`, `id`, `sketch_name`, `centre`, `diagonal`, `body_label`, `time`, `created`, `cache_memo`, `counter_marks`, `counter_face1`, `counter_face2`; a boss also `sketch_id`, `depth`, `corners`, `first`, `merge`, `body_id`, `cache_text`, `counter_favorite`; a cut `target_label`, `target_id`, `target_uid`, `target_created`, `ring`, `counter_end` |
| fillet | `name`, `sketch`, `fillet`, `hole`, `id`, `radius`, `anchor`, `body_label`, `body_id`, `body_regen`, `edges` (fields `label`, `counter`, `faces`), `faces` (fields `counter`, `ring`, `edge`), `closing` (items of `edges`), `dim_edge`, `time`, `created`, `cache_memo`, `counter_marks`; every face entry `kind`, `uid`, `created`, `face` |

`corners` is `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` in metres in the Front Plane's frame (x along model X,
y along model Y), `x0 < x1` and `y0 < y1`; `depth` is the blind depth in metres along
+Z. A circle's `centre` is `[cx, cy]` and its `start` `[cx + r, cy]`, in the same frame. Every listing line reads these by name; nothing in the stream depends on an item's
position except through `$f_index` (the feature id, 15.5.4, and the place number of
15.5.7).

## 15.5.4 Ids, creation numbers, stamps and times

**Feature ids.** Once the part has a feature, SOLIDWORKS numbers these records in
tree order: Comments, Favorites, the history folder, Selection Sets, Design Binder,
Annotations, Surface Bodies, Solid Bodies, the Lights and Cameras folder of
`Config-0`, Material and Origin get `fixed.id_base + K` for K 0 to 10, and the
design's items get `fixed.id_base + 11 + $f_index`. The others keep the template's
101. In the empty part every record has 101. `doc.stamp` (15.1.2.1) is one more than the
last id given: 102 in o01 (101 + 1), 120 in o02, o03, o07 (107 + 11 + 2), 140 in o12
(125 + 11 + 4), 150 in o13, 130 in o11 (113 + 11 + 6, six features) and 132 in o10
(118 + 11 + 3: its import, its circle and its cut). VERIFIED (8 parts). So
`fixed.id_base` is derived:

    fixed.id_base = doc.stamp - 11 - len(features)

and whatever value `doc.stamp` has, the ids follow it. They must stay above 101, so
`doc.stamp` must be at least `113 + len(features)`; the generator's values (15.6.6:
120 for one extrusion, 140 for two, `100 + 20n` for n, a cut counting as an extrusion and
a fillet not counted) give `fixed.id_base` 107 and 125, the values of o02 and o12, and
`89 + 18n` in general, one less for each fillet (106 for a box with a fillet, 123 for o11's
features). The sketch's tie to the origin repeats the Origin's id, `fixed.id_base +
10` (15.5.7).

**Stamps.** A record carries the version it was made in (17000, build 2024016) when
SOLIDWORKS numbered it, and 0, 0 otherwise. The design's records are always stamped.

**Creation numbers** (`UID`, the u32 after the flags). The template's records use 1
to 25. The design's items count on from 26: a sketch takes the next number and
leaves 5 more unused when it is the part's first sketch and 6 more when it is a later
one; an extrusion, boss or cut, takes the next one. o02: Sketch1 26, Boss-Extrude1 32;
o12: Sketch2 33, Boss-Extrude2 40; o11: Sketch2 (a circle) 35, Cut-Extrude1 42; o10,
whose first sketch is a circle off the origin after an imported body (38): Sketch1 39,
Cut-Extrude1 45. VERIFIED. o10 settles what o02 and o12 left open: the gap follows the
sketch's place, not whether its first corner is the origin. The number appears in the
record, in the history entry, in the face records (15.5.8, 15.5.13) and, plus one, in
D1. After a cut, the last item of a part that has one, `hdr.next_id` is the cut's
number plus one (46 in o10, VERIFIED). A fillet's radius dimension takes the next number
and the fillet the one after: o11's `Fillet1` is 34 after `Boss-Extrude1`'s 32, and its
`D1` holds 33; `Fillet2` is 44 after `Cut-Extrude1`'s 42, its `D1` holding 43 (VERIFIED, the
u32 of 15.5.14). The kit's log shows a first fillet attempt that made no feature before each
of o11's fillets; nothing in the part records it. After a fillet, `hdr.next_id` is the
fillet's number plus one (45 in o11, VERIFIED).

**Times.** Records hold FILETIMEs (high word first) and Unix seconds:

| Where | Slot | Generator writes |
|---|---|---|
| each template record's tail | `fixed.<key>.time` | `doc.filetime` |
| each design record's tail | `$f.time` | `doc.filetime` |
| history entries and extrusion face records | `$f.created` | `doc.created` |
| the extrusion's name record | `doc.created` (VERIFIED equal in o02, o07, o13) | `doc.created` |
| the Origin and every sketch's unit component | `tpl.unix` (15.0.2) | `doc.created` (15.2's rule) |

In the fixtures these are instants of the session (a sketch's `created` is one
second after `doc.created` in o02). That SOLIDWORKS accepts one instant for all is a
HYPOTHESIS; b3 and b4 show it accepts records whose times came from another
document.

## 15.5.5 The planes' display

Each reference plane is drawn as a rectangle around the model. After the plane's
tail come constants, then three doubles, the plane's label corner; then
`moDefaultRefPlnData_c` with the plane's orientation (constants), four doubles, the
rectangle's half sizes `hu, -hu, hv, -hv` in the plane's frame, 6 zero bytes, two
i32 -4, a u8 0, three doubles, the rectangle's centre, then u32 1 and 12 zero bytes:

| Plane | (hu, hv) | label corner | centre |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front | (hx, hy) | (cx - hu, cy + hv, 0) | (cx, cy, 0) |
| Top | (hx, hz) | (cx - hu, 0, cz - hv) | (cx, 0, cz) |
| Right | (hz, hy) | (0, cy + hv, cz + hu) | (0, cy, cz) |

with `model.centre` = `(cx, cy, cz)` = `(min + max)/2` and, for each axis,
`e = max - min` and `h = e/2 + e/2*0.1` (half the extent and a tenth of that,
computed in that order). The empty part has `model.centre` (0, 0, 0) and `hu` 0.08899,
`hv` 0.05500000000000001 (the double the fixture holds) for all three planes. The
listing writes the label corners and rectangles as expressions over
`$planes.<plane>.hu`, `$planes.<plane>.hv` and `$model.centre`. VERIFIED bit for bit
(o01, o02, o03, o07, o12; the rule also gives the values o11 and o13 hold). These are caches: a1
left them at the old depth and rebuilt correctly (`docs/NATIVE_SLDPRT_WRITER.md` 5).
The margin for a very thin model, where SOLIDWORKS may have a minimum size, is
UNKNOWN.

## 15.5.6 The design's features

`@for f in $features` writes, for a sketch (`$f.sketch`),
`config-0-resolvedfeatures-circle.srl` when `$f.circle` and
`config-0-resolvedfeatures-sketch.srl` otherwise; for a fillet (`$f.fillet`)
`config-0-resolvedfeatures-fillet.srl`; and for an extrusion
`config-0-resolvedfeatures-cut.srl` when `$f.cut` and
`config-0-resolvedfeatures-extrude.srl` otherwise. Class records
are counted by the archive, so a class's first object anywhere in the archive is a
class record and later ones are references; the listings never decide that
themselves. Back-references are `objref`s to labels:

| Label | Object | Set in | Referred to by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `$f.name` of a sketch | its `moProfileFeature_c` | 15.5.7, 15.5.12 | the extrusion's child list |
| `$f.name` of an extrusion | its `moExtrusion_c` or `moICE_c` | 15.5.8, 15.5.13 | its two box records |
| `$f.name` of a fillet | its `Fillet_c` | 15.5.14 | the fillet's own face entries |
| `$f.body_label`, `$f.target_label` | the body's `moAtom_c` in `Config-0` | 15.4.6 | the extrusion; a cut names the body it makes and the one it uses up; a fillet the body it changes |
| `ext_obj` | `moExtObject_c` of the first extrusion | 15.5.8 | every face record of every extrusion, cut and fillet |
| `$e.label` of a fillet's edge | the `moEdgeRef_c` of its first list of edges | 15.5.14 | the fillet's edge components, and the edge its dimension is drawn on (`$f.dim_edge`) |
| `face_end1`, `side1` to `side4`, `face_end2`, `side5` to `side8` | the extrusion's face id records | 15.5.8 | its two face-reference lists |
| `cyl`, `cap0`, `cap1`, `ring0` to `ring4` | a cut's face entries in its second and third lists | 15.5.13 | its last two face-reference lists |
| `ringa0` to `ringa4` | a cut's face entries in its first list | 15.5.13 | nothing; every entry of a cut's lists is labelled |
| `notes`, `notes_end`, `env_folder`, `sketch_blocks` | template objects | 15.5.3, 15.4 | template objects |

Labels are names, so feature names must be unique and must not be one of the other
labels; the names of 15.6.2 are, and a fillet's edge labels (`Fillet1.e1` ...) are the only
labels with a full stop in them. VERIFIED: the u16s that move with the archive
between o02 and o12 (whose archive indices differ by 1 to 8 at these places) are
these `objref`s, but for the sketch's `regen` counter, which moved by the same 1 by
chance (o13 shows it is not a reference); the listings give both parts byte for
byte.

## 15.5.8 A blind boss extrusion

The first extrusion of a part (`$f.first`) and the later ones differ up to their
first box record; from there one listing serves both, with `@if $f.merge` for the
two places they differ after it.

| Part | First extrusion | Later extrusion |
|---|---|---|
| class | `moExtrusion_c`, labelled `$f.name` | `moICE_c`, labelled `$f.name` |
| node | `nodehead` (flags 0x40000140, UID `$f.id`), one child: `objref $f.sketch_name` | the same |
| tail | `feattail`, id `fixed.id_base + 11 + $f_index`, stamped, `$f.cache_memo`, `$f.time` | the same |
| after the tail | constants, `objref $f.body_label`, u32 `$f.body_id`, constants | the same |
| face records | `moPerBodyChooserData_c`, `moFaceRef_c`, `moEndFaceSurfIdRep_c`, then `moFR_c` whose first object is `moExtObject_c` (labelled `ext_obj`): an empty `moCStringHandle_c` and one holding `doc.name`, u16 2, u8 0, `doc.created`, empty strings, then u32 `$f.id` and `$f.created`; then four `moFromSktEntSurfIdRep_c` (one per side) each with a `moFR_c`: `objref ext_obj`, u32 `$f.id`, u32 `$f.created`, u32 the side 4, 3, 2, 1; then a second `moFaceRef_c` with its end face and four sides the same way | the 3Int classes `moEndFace3IntSurfIdRep_c` and `moFromSktEnt3IntSurfIdRep_c`; each `moFR_c` refers to the first extrusion's `ext_obj` and adds i32 -1, u32 0; the two `moFaceRef_c` carry the free counters `counter_face1` and `counter_face2` |
| box | `moBBoxCenterData_c`: u32 1, `$f.centre`, `$f.diagonal`, 8 zero bytes, u32 1 when `merge`, 6 zero bytes, `objref $f.name`, constants, `objref $f.name` | the same without the u32 1 |

Then, for both: `moPerBodyChooserData_c`, two `moFaceRef_c` whose lists are the
`objref`s `face_end1, side1 ... side4` and `face_end2, side5 ... side8`, the second
box record (as the first, with one `objref $f.name`), `moPMarkRecord_c` with u16
`counter_marks` and zero bytes, `moEndSpec_c` (constants; blind is the only end
condition seen), the dimension (`moDisplayDistanceDim_c`, `moFeatureDimHandle_c`,
`ParallelPlaneDistanceDim_c`), the parameter `moLengthParameter_c` named `D1`,
`moFavoriteHandle_c` and `moFromEndSpec_c`. The display object and its handle are
`dimdisplay` in `config-0-macros.srl`, which a fillet's radius dimension shares with the
kind 20 (15.5.14); one of its fields is the number of the annotation view the dimension is
drawn in, 200, the `*Top` view (15.4.9; VERIFIED: o11's `Fillet1`, drawn in its `*Back` view,
holds 201 there and the kind 20 where the extrusion holds 18, and is otherwise the extrusion's
display byte for byte).

**D1.** The named node `D1`, then the 22 bytes of `docs/NATIVE_SLDPRT_WRITER.md`
4.4, then `$f.depth`: the driving value, which SOLIDWORKS rebuilds from (a1,
VERIFIED). Then a u32 `=$f.sketch_id+1` (27 for Sketch1, 34 for Sketch2), and the
dimension's geometry, all in metres from the rectangle's second corner (x1, y1) and
the depth: (x1, y1); (x1, y1, depth); -x1 and -y1 in a frame; `$f.cache_text`;
-depth twice; depth twice. VERIFIED (o02, o07 at 31 mm, o12's two). The witness
values are caches (a1 left them stale). `cache_text` is x1 times 0.2 in every
fixture to the last bit but one: o02's 0.002 equals `0.01*0.2`, o12's
0.0079999999999999984 is one unit below `0.04*0.2`, so the cases read it; the
generator writes `x1*0.2` (a cache, HYPOTHESIS that the last bit does not matter).

**`moFavoriteHandle_c`** holds the free u32 `counter_favorite` and, near its end, a
u32 `=int($f.merge)`. **`moFromEndSpec_c`** holds 16 bytes that differ between the
first and the later form and are constant within each (o02, o07, o13 and o12 for the
first; o12 for the later): two constants of the specification, HYPOTHESIS that they
are not ids SOLIDWORKS checks.

What `merge` stands for: o12's second extrusion was made with merge off. The fields
that follow `merge` here could as well follow "first extrusion"; the generator only
writes the two combinations the fixtures show (first and merged, later and not
merged), so the question does not arise (15.6).

## 15.5.13 A cut through all

`config-0-resolvedfeatures-cut.srl`: the cut of a circle sketch through all, in both

fillet on its four edges along its depth (15.5.14). The cut makes a new

**Face entries.** The cut's records name faces of the part by the feature that made them.

extrusion's `moExtObject_c`, 15.5.8), the making feature's creation number and its

## 15.5.14 A fillet

`config-0-resolvedfeatures-fillet.srl`: one fillet of constant radius, `$f.radius`, in one of
the two forms o11 shows, which `$f.hole` tells apart:

| Form | The edges it rounds | Where it comes | Body it changes | In o11 |

| `kind` | Class | The face | After the number |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | none (u16 0 alone) | the end of one boundary loop of the face a list is about, before the next loop | |

edge whose "ends" are the far face itself. The box's four edges are, in the fillet's order,
(4, 3), (3, 2), (2, 1) and (1, 4), as 15.5.13 records (VERIFIED, o11). Sides 1 and 4
meet at the rectangle's first corner (x0, y0): the radius dimension is drawn at that corner
