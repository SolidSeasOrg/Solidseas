# 15.1 The container, the XML and text members, the small members

This section gives the container a generated part is written in (15.1.1), the
slots its members use (15.1.2), and the 21 members whose listings it owns:
the OPC package members (15.1.3), the document properties (15.1.4), the XML
tree and keyword index (15.1.5), the document content summary (15.1.6) and
the small binary members (15.1.7). Section 15.1.8 lists what each listing was
proved on and what is still open.

Evidence names fixtures by their prefix (o01 to o19, `docs/FORMAT.md`, where
the evidence comes from). The parts the generator's designs correspond to are
o01 (empty), o02 (one box), o03 (o02 saved again), o04 (o02 with three file
custom properties), o07 (o02's box at 31 mm) and o12 (two boxes, the second
extruded with merge off). o14's typed properties also prove the property
elements of `custom.xml` (15.1.4). Other fixtures are used only to say what a
field means.

## 15.1.1 The container

The generator writes the 2015+ container of `docs/FORMAT.md` section 2 with
`solidseas.nativewrite.container.build_container`, passing a `Member` per
member and the key and signatures below. `build_container` writes the 8-byte
header (the key, then `00 00 00 04`), the local headers and data, the central
directory and the end record, every stored offset relative to byte 8, every
name nibble-swapped, with no decoys.

### 15.1.1.1 Key and signatures

Let R[i] be the low byte of output i of the MSVC `rand()` (`docs/FORMAT.md`
2.7): seed 1, `state = state * 214013 + 2531011 mod 2^32`, output
`(state >> 16) & 0x7FFF`, R[0] the first output. The generator:

1. picks k uniformly at random from 1 to 999 (an identity value, made up for
   each file);
2. writes key = R[16k], R[16k+1], R[16k+2], R[16k+3];
3. writes the local-header signature R[16k+7], R[16k+6], R[16k+5], R[16k+4],
   the directory signature R[16k+11] down to R[16k+8], and the end-record
   signature R[16k+15] down to R[16k+12], and passes them to
   `build_container` as `signatures`.

VERIFIED: all 19 fixtures follow this rule, with k from 2 to 990, and k
differs between documents of one session. How SOLIDWORKS picks k is UNKNOWN;
SOLIDWORKS opened the candidate x1, whose key and signatures follow the rule
(`docs/NATIVE_SLDPRT_WRITER.md` 6.1).

The generator computes the signatures from the sequence and does not leave
them to `container.default_signatures(key)`. That function recovers the
generator state from the key alone, and for 24 of the 999 keys R gives at
k = 1 to 999 two states fit the key and it returns the wrong one (VERIFIED by
running it on every k). For the other 975 it gives the same signatures.

### 15.1.1.2 Members, order and directory fields

The generator writes the 35 members of the table in section 15.0.1, in that
order. The order is the members' names sorted by their UTF-8 bytes, which is
how every fixture orders its directory (VERIFIED, 19 fixtures, one sorted run
each). Every member gets these directory fields:

| Field | Value | Kind | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| version needed, flags, method | 20, `0x0006`, 8 | constant | `build_container` writes them; VERIFIED (`docs/FORMAT.md` 2.3) |
| "time" (`dos_time`) | `0xB6C4` for `Header2`, `0xB719` for every other member | constant | see below |
| "date" (`dos_date`) | `0x1A6D` for every member | constant | see below |
| CRC-32, sizes | of the member's bytes | derived | `build_container` computes them |
| internal attributes (`internal_attr`) | 1 when the member is text by the rule below, else 0 | derived | VERIFIED, 773 of 773 fixture members |
| external attributes, extra, comment | 0, none, none | constant | `build_container` writes them |

**Time and date.** The two fields are opaque (`docs/FORMAT.md` 2.9). On
13 parts (o01 to o07, o09 to o13 and o17) they are the same in every
member, `0xB719` and `0x1A6D`, except `Header2` and `Preview`, which carry
`0xB6C4` and `0x1A6D`. They are not per save: o02 and o03 have the same
fields. They are not per document kind either: o14, a part of the same
session, has `0x3719` and `0x1AA9` (`0x36C4` and `0x1AE9` for `Header2` and
`Preview`), the assemblies o08 and o15 have date `0x1A72`, and the drawings
`0x1A28` and `0x1A68`. Their rule is UNKNOWN. The generator writes the 13
parts' values (`Preview` is not written). SOLIDWORKS opens a part with these
values: the candidate x1 is o02 laid out again with them (VERIFIED,
`docs/NATIVE_SLDPRT_WRITER.md` 6.1). Whether other values are accepted is
not known and does not need to be.

**Internal attributes.** The value is the text-or-binary verdict of zlib's
`data_type` detection (zlib's documented `Z_TEXT`, which PKWARE APPNOTE 4.4.14
stores as bit 0, "apparently an ASCII or text file"), taken over the whole
member: 1 when the member holds no byte from 0 to 6, 14 to 25 or 28 to 31 and
at least one byte that is 9, 10, 13 or 32 to 255; otherwise 0. An empty
member is 0. VERIFIED: the rule gives the stored value for all 773 members of
the 19 fixtures, including the UTF-16 `MATERIALTREE` (0, it holds zero
bytes) and `KeyWords` (1, its first byte is `0x86`). On a generated part it
gives 1 for the 8 XML members and `swXmlContents/Features` and
`swXmlContents/KeyWords`, and 0 for the other 25.

**Compression.** Each member's data is raw deflate made by
`container.deflate_member`: zlib level 1, window bits -15, memLevel 8,
default strategy; an empty member is the two bytes `03 00`. VERIFIED (773
fixture members compress to their stored bytes this way, `docs/FORMAT.md`
2.9).

**No decoys.** The generator writes no decoy units and no filler. Every
fixture has decoys, but SOLIDWORKS opened, rebuilt, edited and saved o02
laid out again without them (candidate x1, VERIFIED).

## 15.1.2 Slots

The listings of this section use the shared slots (README 15.0.2) `doc.name`,
`doc.iso`, `doc.author`, `doc.created`, `doc.changed`, `doc.stamp`,
`doc.stamp2`, `features`, `bodies`, `props` and `props_by_name`, and the slots
below under `meta.`.
Section 15.6 gives the rule or the generator's value for each.

**Escaping.** Every `${...}` in an XML listing of this section is replaced
by the slot's value with `&` written as `&amp;`, `<` as `&lt;`, `>` as `&gt;`
and `"` as `&quot;`, in that order of precedence (`&` first). SOLIDWORKS
writes `<` and `>` this way in attributes (VERIFIED, every fixture's
`Material &lt;not specified&gt;`); `&` and `"` follow XML 1.0 and are a
HYPOTHESIS. The two fields of a property item written with `utf8`,
`$p.xml_value` and `$p.xml_text`, are not `${...}` and are not escaped again:
section 15.6.9 gives them already escaped, the way SOLIDWORKS escapes element
text. Non-ASCII text is written as UTF-8, unescaped (VERIFIED, o17,
whose file name is Cyrillic). Numbers are written in decimal with no sign
for positive values and no leading zeros.

## 15.1.3 The package members

`[Content_Types].xml` and `_rels/.rels` are the OPC package's content types
and relationships (ECMA-376 Part 2). Both are constant: the same bytes in all
19 fixtures (VERIFIED). Listings `content-types.srl` and `rels.srl`.

| Member | Bytes |
|---|---|
| `[Content_Types].xml` | the declaration `<?xml version="1.0"?>` (no encoding), CR LF, the `Types` element on one line, CR LF. No BOM. 556 bytes |
| `_rels/.rels` | the declaration with `encoding="UTF-8" standalone="yes"`, CR LF, the `Relationships` element on one line (rId3 app.xml, rId2 core.xml, rId4 custom.xml, in that order), CR LF. 597 bytes |

The other docProps members are not named by the package; SOLIDWORKS reads
them by name.

Every XML member of sections 15.1.3 to 15.1.5 has this shape: an XML
declaration, CR LF, the root element and everything in it on one line, CR LF
(two CR LF pairs in all, the second ending the member). None has a BOM.
VERIFIED on every XML member of the 5 parts.

## 15.1.4 Document properties

All six are UTF-8 with the declaration
`<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8" standalone="yes"?>`.

**`docProps/app.xml`** (`app.srl`). OPC extended properties: `Template`
`Normal.dotm`, `TotalTime` (`meta.total_time`), `Application` `SOLIDWORKS`,
`DocSecurity` 0, `Company` (`meta.company`), `LinksUpToDate`, `SharedDoc`
and `HyperlinksChanged` `false`, `AppVersion` `23.0000`, in that order. The
whole member is the same in all 19 fixtures (VERIFIED); `AppVersion` does not
follow the saving version (`docs/FORMAT.md` 7.6). The generator replaces the
vendor's company name with `SolidSeas` and the inherited editing time with
`0`. That SOLIDWORKS accepts them is a HYPOTHESIS; neither is read for
anything the fixtures show.

**`docProps/core.xml`** (`core.srl`). OPC core properties with one save
record: `dc:lastModifiedBy` (`doc.author`), `dcterms:created`
(`meta.created_iso`), `dcterms:modified` (`doc.iso`). A document saved more
than once in other sessions accumulates records (`docs/FORMAT.md` 11.7); a
generated part has one, as the 5 parts do. The generator writes `SolidSeas`
as the last saver (HYPOTHESIS that it is accepted: every fixture names an
account; the field is free text).

**`docProps/custom.xml`** (`custom.srl`). File custom properties
(`docs/FORMAT.md` 11.1). Two property sections. `DocumentSummaryInformation`
holds the code page (`vt:i2` 65001, pid 1, `TypeID` 0), pid 22 `vt:bool` `No`
and an empty name dictionary entry (pid 0). `UserDefinedProperties` holds the
code page and the empty dictionary entry, then one dictionary entry per
property in `props_by_name` order (`name`, `pid`), then one `property`
element per property in `props` order:

- a text property: `<property name="N" pid="P" IsEquation="False" TypeID="30">`;
  any other: the same without `IsEquation`, with its `type_id` (3, 64 or 11);
- the value element `vt:<vt>` holding `xml_value`;
- `FPVals`, holding a second `vt:<vt>` element with `xml_text`, the value as
  typed;
- `</property>`.

With no properties the member is constant: the same bytes in o01, o02, o03,
o07, o12, o13 and o17 (VERIFIED). With properties: o04 byte for byte (two
text properties and a whole number, pids 3 to 5 in the order they were
added, the dictionary by name), and every element and dictionary entry of
o14's five typed properties (text, date, whole number, real number, yes),
with its dictionary entries in o14's order (VERIFIED). o04 and o05 (o04 with
a material) hold the same bytes. The dictionary is ordered by name without
regard to case, as `ISolidWorksInformation.xml`'s is (15.6.9).

Where the elements go: a part saved once has its dictionary first and every
property after it (o04). o14, whose five typed properties were added before a
first save and its two linked ones before a second, holds the five before
its dictionary and the two after it, so a property already saved once is
written before the dictionary. HYPOTHESIS (two parts); the generator writes
every property as new, after the dictionary, the form o04 proves.
`IsEquation` is written on text properties only, as on all of o04's and
o14's (VERIFIED, 10 properties).

**`docProps/Config-0-Properties.xml`** (`config-0-properties.srl`).
Properties of configuration 0, `Default`. With none: an empty
`DocumentSummaryInformation` section and a `UserDefinedProperties` section

**`docProps/OpenTime.xml`** (`opentime.srl`). Open counters: `count` 0,

## 15.1.6 `SwDocContentMgr/SwDocContentMgrInfo`

read without loading the model: materials, the active configuration, the

count and then, per configuration, its name (`cstr`) and the value. o13 (two

`Default`. In order:

The value of map 5 is a u32 count of the bodies still in the part
(`live_bodies`: a body a cut has used up is not listed) and, when it is not 0,
the count again and then per body its u32 `id` and `cstr` `name`, the name of the
feature that made it. The value of map 6 is the same count and, when not 0, the
count again and per body its u32 `id` and a u32 1 (not decoded). VERIFIED: o01
